## Scenario
Communication project\
You're working with an AI assistant to draft a series of emails for a marketing campaign.

- Delegation: What aspects of this project would you handle yourself vs. collaborate on with AI?
- Description: How would you communicate your vision for the campaign's tone, purpose, and success criteria to the AI?
- Discernment: What criteria would help you evaluate whether the AI-drafted emails meet your needs?
- Diligence: What considerations around transparency and responsibility would be important?

## Solution
### Delegation
#### Tasks by AI 
- Draft emails for the different stages of interaction with the user 
- Write an outline for each email
- Write out the contents of the email

#### Tasks by user 
- Identify who the target audience is
- Provide the AI with the context of the campaign, including tone and purpose
- Find the target audience through social media platforms

### Description
You're going to help me draft a series of emails for a marketing campaign on X. The tone of the campaign is emotional and empathic. The purpose is to promote awareness of X so that we can get the financial support to achieve Y. The success criteria is A, B and C. Your output should be in the format for an actual email draft

### Discernment
- Formal and empathic tone
- Higher conversion rates

### Diligence
- Mentioning who the beneficial party is and how the funds will be used
