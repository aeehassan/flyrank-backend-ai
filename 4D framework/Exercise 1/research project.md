## Scenario
Research project\
You're using AI to help analyze a large dataset for a research paper.

- Delegation: How would you divide the analytical work between yourself and the AI?
- Description: What context would the AI need to understand about your research question to do its share of the tasks well?
- Discernment: How would you verify the AI's analysis for accuracy?
- Diligence: What ethical considerations might arise when publishing AI-assisted research?
## Solution
### Delegation
#### Me
- Study dataset description
- Conduct data cleaning and preprocessing

#### AI
- Create different types of plots to convey different meanings
- Draft analysis and discussion sections of the paper

### Description
Based on the delegation:
You're going to help me analyze a large dataset for a research paper. The topic of research is X. I want you to come up with these types of plots: A, B and C, along with any other one common in this domain. Next off, I want you to draft an analysis and discussions of the paper based on these results. I want you to follow the approach common to research papers in this domain. Be as specific as possible in your output each subsection so I can easily compile the final. Your output should be in a format that is easy to copy and paste into a document: Plots first and Draft second. Please provide references for any sources you use in your analysis and discussion sections.

### Discernment
I would do so by: 
- Checking the plots for accuracy and clarity
- Reviewing the analysis and discussion sections for logical consistency 
- Proper interpretation of results, and alignment with the research question
- Cross-referencing the analysis style with other research papers in the same domain 

### Diligence
- Ensuring that the AI's contributions are properly cited in the research paper
- Being held accountable for the final content of the paper, including any errors or misinterpretations that may arise from the AI's analysis
